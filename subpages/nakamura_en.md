# Nakamura Laboratory

**The University of Electro-Communications (UEC), Tokyo, Japan**

The Nakamura Laboratory conducts research in **artificial intelligence, robotics, machine learning, and intelligent systems**. Our research focuses on understanding and developing intelligent systems through machine learning, probabilistic modeling, and robotics.

## Research Areas

Our main research interests include:

- Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
- Intelligent Robotics
- Symbol Emergence and Emergent Communication
- Multimodal Learning
- Probabilistic Generative Models
- Human and Robot Motion Analysis
- Multi-Agent Systems

We aim to develop intelligent systems that can autonomously acquire concepts, symbols, and knowledge through interaction with their environment and other agents.

## Research Group

The Nakamura Laboratory is a research group at **The University of Electro-Communications** led by **Principal Investigator Tomoaki Nakamura**.

Our group consists of faculty members, researchers, doctoral students, master's students, and undergraduate students working on research in artificial intelligence and robotics.
